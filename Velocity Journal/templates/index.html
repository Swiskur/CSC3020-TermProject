About Velocity Journal

The velocity journal is used to help pitchers track their throwing speed and document their progress over time. Our goal is to help athletes improve their performance and reach their full potential on the mound. This page is free to use to provide a place for athletes of any age or skill level to track their velocity and journal about their journey. Whether you're a beginner looking to improve your skills or an experienced pitcher aiming to reach new heights, the velocity journal is here to support you along the way and help you achieve your goals! Who knows, maybe one day you'll be throwing heat like Jacob deGrom is here below!


Get Started

Use the Velocity Tracker to log your speed for each day you are radar gunned. This way you can see your progression. Then, you can reate journal entries to reflect on your progress as you climb to the top of your pitching staff! Use these journal entries to note what drills you did that day, how you felt, and any other observations you made during your training. And it's that easy and free to use!
